# Code of Conduct

Our community is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and professional environment.

Participants should be considerate, provide constructive technical feedback, respect differing experience levels and backgrounds, and avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, doxxing, and threats.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation, or block accounts when necessary. Enforcement is at the maintainers' discretion, applying rules consistently and in good faith.

For conduct issues, contact repository maintainers through a private channel listed on their verified GitHub profiles; do not expose confidential reports in public issues. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
